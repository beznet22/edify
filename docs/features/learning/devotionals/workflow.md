# Devotional routine workflow (Devotionals view)

> Multi-step process for engaging with a generated devotional: read the reflection, study the passage, respond, and save insights. The Devotionals view of the daily devotional rhythm.

This workflow is owned by the Devotionals capability (`docs/features/learning/devotionals/`). The Personal Bible Study view of the same workflow lives in `docs/features/intelligence/personal-bible-study/workflow.md` — that view emphasizes Scripture reading and note-taking; this view emphasizes engaging with the generated reflection content.

---

# Trigger

The user opens Edify and sees "Today's Devotional" on the home screen. The devotional was generated by the Devotional Agent earlier (typically overnight, before the user's preferred devotional time).

Preconditions:

- The Devotional Agent has generated a devotional
- The user has at least one Bible translation installed locally
- The user has a preferred devotional time configured (default: morning)

---

# Steps

### 1. See the devotional on the home screen

You open Edify. The home screen appears. At the top is the "Today's Devotional" card showing:

- A passage reference (e.g., "Romans 8:1")
- A one-line summary of the reflection
- A "Read" button

The card is glanceable; you see what's offered without committing to engage.

**If you want a different devotional**: tap "New Devotional" to request a fresh one. The current devotional is marked as `superseded` and a new one is generated (async; takes a few seconds to a few minutes).

**If no devotional is available**: the home screen shows a "Generate Devotional" button. Tapping it dispatches the Devotional Agent; the devotional appears when ready.

### 2. Open the devotional

You tap "Read" on the devotional card. The devotional detail screen loads with:

- The full passage text (in your preferred translation)
- The reflection (Markdown, with citations)
- A prayer prompt
- A reflection question

The devotional transitions from `due` to `read`. A StudySession is created (or attached to the current session).

### 3. Read the reflection

You read the reflection. The reflection cites the passage and (when relevant) your own past notes. The reflection is honest about what the text says and frames theological implications as applications, not as authoritative claims.

**If the reflection feels off**: you can rate it (thumbs down + optional comment). The Devotional Agent uses feedback to inform future devotionals. You can also tap "New Devotional" to skip to a different one.

### 4. Study the passage

You tap the cited passage. The Bible reader opens at the passage. You can:

- Read the surrounding verses for context
- Tap cross-references to explore related passages
- Compare the passage in another translation (if installed)
- Add a note to the passage

**If the devotional's passage is in a chapter you're studying**: the devotional session is attached to your ongoing study session; the devotional's notes integrate with your study notes.

### 5. Engage with the prayer prompt

You consider the prayer prompt. You may:

- Pray silently (no UI action)
- Type a prayer as a note (attached to the devotional session)
- Use voice-to-text if available (Phase 2+)

The note is part of the StudySession transcript and is searchable in the KG.

### 6. Engage with the reflection question

You consider the reflection question. You may:

- Type a personal response as a note
- Skip the question (some days the user just reads and prays)
- Save the question for later reflection

The devotional transitions from `read` to `reflected` when you add a note (or you can stay in `read` if you don't add one).

### 7. Optionally ask the Study Agent

If a question arises during your reading, you can tap "Ask" to open a chat with the Study Agent (per `docs/features/intelligence/ai-bible-chat/`). The question is grounded in the current devotional context.

### 8. End the devotional session

When you're done, you tap "Done" or simply close Edify. The session transitions to `ended`. The Knowledge Agent may run async to:

- Link the devotional to related concepts and sermons (KG enrichment)
- Suggest a related passage for tomorrow
- Mark related nodes in the KG as "recently engaged"

**If you close the app without tapping "Done"**: the session is preserved in its last-known state. On return, you can resume or end.

### 9. Rate the devotional (optional)

You may rate the devotional (thumbs up/down + optional comment). The feedback is used to inform future devotionals. The devotional's quality is assessed both by your engagement (did you read it? did you add a note?) and by your explicit feedback.

---

# Outcome

After a successful run, you have:

- Engaged with a Scripture passage in a reflective way
- Read a personal reflection grounded in your study history
- Optionally added a note (prayer, response, or insight)
- A devotional session persisted in your Knowledge Graph
- Tomorrow's devotional already waiting (if the Devotional Agent ran on schedule)

The daily devotional rhythm is supported without feeling forced or performative.

---

# Failure paths

| Failure | You see | Recovery |
|---------|---------|----------|
| No devotional yet | "Generate Devotional" button | Tap to generate; devotional appears when ready |
| Devotional generation failed | "Devotional unavailable" | Try "New Devotional"; if persistent, notify support |
| Translation not installed | Install prompt | Requires network |
| Study Agent unavailable | "Ask unavailable" | You can still read and reflect without AI |
| App closed mid-devotional | Session preserved on return | You resume or end |

No failure loses your devotional content.

---

# Persistence

What is saved at session end:

- **StudySession** — the devotional session
- **Devotional** — the generated content (already persisted when scheduled)
- **Note(s)** — your prayers, responses, insights
- **Read state** — devotional marked as read in the KG
- **Feedback** — your rating, if provided
- **Concept links** — inferred from your notes (Knowledge Agent)

All persisted to `kg.db`. Cross-device sync per `synchronization.md`.

# Cross-feature touchpoints

This workflow depends on:

- **personal-bible-study** — for verse reading and cross-references
- **ai-bible-chat** — for the Study Agent when you tap "Ask"
- **live-sermon-engine** — past sermons are linked from the devotional
- **peer-sync** — devotional sessions sync to other devices
- **control-plane** — sync metadata only; devotional content does not leave the device
